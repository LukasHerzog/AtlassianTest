# New Hire Mobile App — Requirements

> Based on Jira issue [HR-63](https://sandboxlh.atlassian.net/browse/HR-63): *Improvement to our New Hire Mobile App*
>
> **Status:** In Progress | **Priority:** Critical | **Assignee:** Lukas Herzog

---

## Overview

A mobile app designed to help new hires get up to speed quickly from day one. The app serves as a single, centralized "start here" experience — replacing the need to dig through scattered emails and documents.

The goal is to make new hires feel **less lost and more confident** during their first days and weeks at the company.

---

## Requirements

### 1. 📋 Onboarding Checklist
- Display a prioritized checklist of tasks for the new hire to complete
- Tasks should include (but are not limited to):
  - Paperwork and administrative setup
  - Tools and accounts to configure
  - People to meet and introductions to make
- Each task should have a **due date** or time frame (e.g., "Day 1", "Week 1")
- New hires should be able to mark tasks as complete
- Progress should be visually indicated (e.g., progress bar or completion percentage)

### 2. 📚 Quick Reference Information
- Provide easy access to key company information, including:
  - **Policies** (e.g., leave, code of conduct, expense)
  - **Benefits** (e.g., health, perks, allowances)
  - **Office info** (e.g., locations, floor maps, facilities)
  - **Tools & systems** (e.g., how to access Jira, Confluence, Slack, etc.)
- Content should be searchable and organized by category
- Information should be kept up to date and easy to maintain

### 3. 🏢 Who's Who / Org Structure
- Help new hires understand the company and team structure
- Display key people the new hire should know (e.g., their manager, teammates, HR contacts)
- Include basic profiles: name, role, team, and contact info
- Optionally show an org chart or team hierarchy view

### 4. 🔔 Nudges & Smart Tips
- Proactively surface contextual tips and reminders to guide the new hire
- Examples:
  - "You haven't completed your tax paperwork yet — it's due tomorrow!"
  - "Week 2 tip: Schedule a 1:1 with your manager this week."
- Notifications or in-app prompts should be timely and relevant to the new hire's onboarding stage

---

## Goals & Success Criteria

- New hires can find everything they need **without relying on email or separate documents**
- Onboarding checklist completion rate improves
- New hires report feeling more confident and prepared from day one
- Reduced volume of repetitive HR questions from new hires

---

## Out of Scope (for initial version)

- Integration with payroll systems
- Custom onboarding flows per department (can be a future enhancement)
- Multi-language support (future consideration)
